When Does Ramadan Start?

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic lunar calendar (Hijri). Fasting from dawn to sunset during Ramadan is one of the Five Pillars of Islam, observed by more than 1.9 billion Muslims globally.

Because the lunar calendar is roughly 11 days shorter than the Gregorian solar calendar, the date shifts backward every year. The official declaration of Ramadan relies on the physical sighting of the new crescent moon (Hilal).
